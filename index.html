How we help

A practical path through a complex industrial problem.

When procurement, technical requirements, and delivery depend on several people, we bring the work into one coordinated plan.

01 / PROCUREMENT

Find a workable supply option.

We clarify requirements, investigate local and international sourcing options, compare suppliers, and manage purchasing and delivery around your project needs.

02 / EQUIPMENT SUPPORT

Bring the right specialists together.

We coordinate the engineering input, suppliers, manufacturing, testing, and installation resources needed to support a replacement, modification, or equipment issue.

03 / PROJECT MANAGEMENT

Keep the whole job moving.

We establish scope, responsibilities, milestones, and dependencies, then track commitments and coordinate decisions through the agreed work.

Qualified providers perform engineering, manufacturing, testing, and installation as required. Proxy Industrial manages the coordination, with technical approvals and customer decisions assigned to the appropriate owners.

Where the solution calls for custom parts, sourcing can include CNC machining, castings, and forgings. Quality requirements, inspections, and documentation are agreed for the project and verified through the relevant providers.

When to call us

A missing part.
A stalled project.
Too many moving pieces.

  • A critical component is unavailable, obsolete, or difficult to source, and you need a practical way forward.

How we work

From the first question to the final handover.

Start with the problem as you understand it. You do not need a finished specification or drawing to begin a conversation.

  1. 01

    Understand

    Clarify the problem, desired outcome, constraints, and people who need to be involved.

  2. 02

    Assess options

    Investigate practical routes and compare requirements, costs, timing, and trade-offs.

  3. 03

    Agree the plan

    Set the scope, responsibilities, schedule, approval points, and completion criteria together.

  4. 04

    Coordinate

    Track progress, connect the right people, and make open issues and required decisions visible.

  5. 05

    Close out

    Coordinate verification against the agreed criteria and bring together relevant handover documentation.
