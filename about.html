Industrial problems often cross several boundaries: procurement, engineering, manufacturing, and work on site. Proxy Industrial brings those activities together, giving you one primary contact to manage the agreed scope and keep the work moving.

Based in Ontario, Canada, Proxy Industrial is led by Managing Director Serhat Duzcu, PMP®. With degrees in Mechanical Engineering and Engineering Management and over a decade of experience overseeing heavy industrial projects for global OEMs, Serhat brings technical understanding and project-management discipline to your problem.

Serhat works across engineers, purchasing teams, suppliers, and technicians to turn requirements into practical options and an executable plan. That means tracking commitments, coordinating decisions, and making sure stakeholders know what is needed and when.

We consider local and international sourcing options to suit the project. Proxy Industrial manages the process, while qualified providers perform the engineering, manufacturing, testing, and installation required. We agree responsibilities, approval points, and completion criteria with you, then coordinate delivery through handover.
