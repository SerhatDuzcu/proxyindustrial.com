Based in Ontario, Canada, Proxy Industrial is led by Serhat Duzcu. With over a decade of experience managing heavy industrial projects for global equipment manufacturers, Serhat brings together the people, suppliers, and resources needed to move a project forward.

Industrial problems rarely sit neatly within one discipline. A purchasing decision may need engineering input. A replacement component may require manufacturing, testing, and installation. Someone needs to connect those activities and follow through.

That is the role Proxy Industrial takes on. You work directly with Serhat to clarify the need, assess practical options, and establish a plan. Throughout the agreed work, you have a reliable contact who tracks commitments, coordinates decisions, and keeps you informed about progress and what is needed next.

Engineering, manufacturing, testing, and installation are carried out by the appropriate qualified providers. Proxy Industrial coordinates their contributions around your requirements, drawing on local and international sourcing options as needed.
