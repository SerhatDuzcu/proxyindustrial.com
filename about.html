Proxy Industrial Inc. was founded to solve a critical bottleneck in the North American supply chain: the gap between high domestic manufacturing costs and the risk of managing overseas suppliers.

Based in Ontario, Canada, Proxy Industrial is led by Managing Director Serhat Duzcu, PMP®. Having Mechanical Engineering and Engineering Management degrees, Serhat brings over a decade of experience overseeing heavy industrial projects for global OEMs.

We act as your local, risk-free proxy. By partnering exclusively with heavily vetted, ISO 9001:2015 certified facilities in Turkey's premier industrial zones, we bypass traditional trading companies to deliver direct-to-factory pricing. We handle all blueprint translation, GD&T verification, quality assurance, and DDP logistics locally; delivering tier-one custom components directly to your dock.
